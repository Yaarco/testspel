Asset pack

Weapons, skins, gloves, characters and sounds come from an asset pack: the .zip made by tools/make-asset-pack.sh from your own game install. Choose it to load it. It is kept in this browser, so you only do this once, and it never leaves your computer.

    Game

    With auto hop off, time every jump on the tick you land. With it on, hold jump to hop on every landing. Hold A or D while turning the mouse the same way to gain speed in the air. Surf ramps are too steep to stand on: hold the strafe key toward the ramp and look along it.

    Each weapon sets your run speed: knife 250, most pistols 240, AK-47 215, AWP 200.

    Connection

    1v1 with a friend connects your browsers directly. Some networks block that; then a relay (TURN) server carries the game. Free ones exist: sign up with a TURN provider and paste the server address, username and password here. Only one of you needs it.
